Sarflog quick reference

Choose the schedule, not the product label

QuestionChooseExamples
Is one final payable total known, with no interest math to reproduce?Fixed totalBNPL, nasiya, store installments, fixed-price service
Is this a supported fixed-rate, fixed-payment loan on declining principal?Standard amortizationMany mortgages, auto loans, personal loans
Does the provider already define exact or irregular rows?Exact contract rowsBalloon, milestones, negotiated plan, custom bank schedule

Keep these axes separate

product kind ≠ schedule strategy
mortgage ≠ automatically standard amortization
bank loan ≠ automatically interest-bearing

Shared output

Every strategy materializes stable schedule rows. The common payment engine then handles allocation, added charges, write-offs, adjustments, archive state, activity, and append-only reversal.

Boundary

Do not claim live calculation parity for revolving balances, variable rates, daily-interest recasting, negative amortization, or income-driven schedules. Record exact provider rows or add a specialized strategy later.
