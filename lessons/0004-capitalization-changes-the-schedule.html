Sarflog architecture lesson 004

Capitalization moves unpaid interest into principal

At the moment of capitalization, the current total owed does not increase. The labels inside that total change—and future interest can then increase because principal is larger.

Recommended v1: record a provider-reported capitalization event and replacement/recalculated schedule. Do not automatically decide when lenders capitalize.

Why the future schedule changes

The ledger invariant must allow reclassification

A rule requiring every ledger entry's total delta to be nonzero would reject capitalization. The correct rule is that at least one component delta is nonzero while their sum still equals total delta.
