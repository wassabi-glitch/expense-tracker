Sarflog architecture lesson 003

Interest rules live inside a calculation engine

Simple versus compound is not another product kind and not another universal Payment Plan choice. It matters only when Sarflog itself calculates interest.

Orientation: the core architecture survives. The standard monthly generator locks one narrow rule; complex interest behavior routes to exact contract until a specialized engine is built.

The tree

PAYMENT PLAN
│
├─ FIXED_TOTAL
│ └─ no interest calculation
│
├─ STANDARD_AMORTIZATION
│ └─ Sarflog calculates monthly interest
│ └─ interest = outstanding principal × monthly rate
│
└─ EXACT_CONTRACT
└─ provider supplies amounts; Sarflog does not calculate interest

Sarflog v1 does not automatically decide what a lender does with unpaid interest after a missed or insufficient payment. That consequence is provider-specific.

What would require expansion later?

A specialized daily-interest or capitalization engine would add contract terms and a calculation strategy. Stable schedule rows, actions, allocations, and ledger history would remain reusable.

Not broken; bounded

The architecture is capable of extension. The current generator intentionally refuses to calculate rules it does not know.
